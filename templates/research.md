# Research starter

## Problem & audience

State the Bitcoin question being investigated, why it matters, and which technical or policy decisions the findings should inform.

## Plan & deliverables

- Publish a written report with sources and methodology.
- Publish data, code, or calculations needed to reproduce conclusions where legally possible.
- Clearly separate evidence, assumptions, and recommendations.

## Verification

1. Read the public report and inspect its source citations.
2. Re-run the supplied calculations or scripts using the documented inputs.
3. Confirm the report answers the stated question and records its limitations.

## Out of scope

Production implementation, investment advice, and claims beyond the documented evidence.
